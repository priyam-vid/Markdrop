## 2. Defects Empirically Demonstrated by Baseline

1. **E1 (Spaces Lost Document-Wide)**:
   In `digital_nospace_kerning.pdf` and `digital_article_2col.pdf`, `TextLayerExtractor` skipped whitespace glyphs and joined text positions with `joinToString("")`, causing tokens like `NeuralDocumentUnderstandingWithoutSpaces` and an overall WER of 95–97%.
2. **E2 (Zero Page Dimensions & Two-Column Interleaving)**:
   In `digital_two_column_real.pdf` and `digital_article_2col.pdf`, `LayoutPage` was instantiated with `width=0f`, preventing `sortLinesInReadingOrder` from triggering the `pageWidth > 200f` branch.
3. **E3 & E4 (Table Recognition Failure)**:
   In `digital_table_multiword.pdf`, `digital_table_heavy.pdf`, and `digital_multipage_table.pdf`, all tables failed completely (Table Cell F1 = 0.00). Word-level gaps and multi-word cells caused the heuristic parser to treat table lines as irregular text.
4. **E13 (Searchable Scan Content Loss)**:
   `scan_image_with_stamp.pdf` (an image scan with a 60-character digital stamp) was accepted as a valid digital text page by the naïve 50-character threshold, losing the full-page scanned image.
